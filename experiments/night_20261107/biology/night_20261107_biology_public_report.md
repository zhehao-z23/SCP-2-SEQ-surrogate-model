Existing sequence-to-distribution predictions provide a useful, limited physical prescreen on the historical development cohort. Learned charge features are a strong comparator: this task does not establish a PLM advantage or biological function classification.

We replayed 30 saved OOF prediction sets (three families, two seeds, five folds; maximum original H discrepancy <1e−16). The cohort contains 21,921 measurement rows, 21,915 distinct teacher sequences and 7,999 protected-relation groups. A fixed lexicographic measurement view represents each exact sequence for ranking; truth distributions are not averaged. The two seeds share the same objects and do not double the sample size.

The target is at least 80% probability mass beyond +5 or −5 in the historical vertex-count63 distribution, which differs from the new area-weighted contract. Whole-bin inclusion/intersection gives conservative bounds without inventing open-tail centers. Lower bound≥0.8 certifies a target; upper bound<0.8 certifies a negative; all other labels remain uncertain. Scores and budgets were frozen before reading the current predictions. This is not the complete historical extreme-shape rule or a Bowley-skewness target.

At a global top220 budget, the 135 certified positive-concentration targets are recovered 124/121 times by charge MLP, 118/117 by residue models and 112/112 by mean models, versus 46 by nominal (K+R−D−E)/length. There are 374 additional uncertain labels. Charge seed1701 selects 124 certified positives, 90 uncertain objects and 6 certified negatives, missing 11 known positives; residue seed1701 selects 118/99/3 and misses 17. Thus certified-target recall is 91.85% versus 87.41%, while the label-identification intervals for true recall remain 29.59–95.11% and 28.78–92.74%. These are not statistical confidence intervals or complete physical recalls.

The annotation-defined RNA context pool contains 3,094 distinct sequences / 565 groups, defined by cached explicit GO:0003723 or a ribosomal-protein name. Missing annotation is not functional absence. Its top 310 budget retrieves 108/108 of 109 certified positive targets with charge and 107/108 with residue, but 287 uncertain labels remain. This pool is family-enriched, not a validated functional-classification benchmark. A positive-potential distribution does not identify a spatial binding interface or establish RNA-binding function; prior electrostatic classification used spatial patch properties and other features. [Shazman and Mandel-Gutfreund, 2008](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1000146)

The practical chain is sequence scoring→fixed-budget candidate selection→structure/physical review→predefined outside-budget checks. Reviewing 220 rather than 21,915 objects reduces the review count by approximately 99%, with explicit known misses; it does not imply the same wall-time saving. Transfer to complete UniProt sequences, AFDB or the new area-weighted labels requires new validation. Isolated-chain labels do not represent native RNA complexes, membranes or cofactors. All case identities, row-level annotations, source-entry snapshots and individual plots remain private.
