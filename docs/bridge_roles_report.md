## Enforced preflight and remaining boundary

The private registry binds source FASTAs, historical groups and manifest, every relationship result, the supplemental exposure audit, role mapping, protected identity lists, guard implementation and physical-input registry. The contract is pinned by SHA-256. Missing files, changed group maps, changed identity lists, unknown requested sequence identities, and incomplete search evidence cause rejection.

While blinded, protected sequence components and aliases are rejected for training, early stopping, model selection, model evaluation, target reads and prediction. Sixteen checks passed, including actual reserved identities, alias exclusion, changed/missing source locks, tampering with protected lists under an unchanged contract pin, and absent final closure. The checks only mutated isolated temporary fixtures.

A pass means that the bridge protection preflight passed for a registered development request. **Existing release eligibility and training/stopping/evaluation role gates remain required.** The guard is a launcher preflight, not an operating-system access-control system; future jobs must require its successful receipt before loading labels or models. The G3 exposure hashes bind the audited snapshot rather than monitoring live remote files. Additional exposure or changed inputs require a new audit and registry.

All final-release requests are rejected by the current registry. Final qualification requires a current complete new-release group map and sequence/profile closure including the protected panel, plus renewed exposure and role approval. The full new-C-to-historical/profile closure has not been performed in this stage. Future release or grouping changes therefore require revalidation, even when exact sequence identities are unchanged.

Public evidence is limited to `summary.json`, `search_summary.json`, `guard_check.json`, `provenance.json`, and generic source. Private candidates, sequences, component bindings, aliases, role tables, labels and predictions are not included.
